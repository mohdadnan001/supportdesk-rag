# Refund Policy
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Digital downloads and subscription services are not eligible for refunds after the first 14 days. To initiate a refund, contact support with your order number and reason for return.

# Password Reset
If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address. The link expires after 1 hour. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support. For security reasons, we cannot reset passwords over the phone.

# Shipping Information
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the continental US. Express shipping delivers within 1-2 business days. International shipping takes 7-14 business days depending on the destination country. Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Free standard shipping is available on orders over $50.

# Account Deletion
To delete your account, go to Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. All personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days. Order history and invoices will be retained for tax purposes as required by law. Account deletion cannot be undone.

# Billing Dispute
If you notice an incorrect charge, contact billing support within 60 days of the transaction. Provide your order ID, the disputed amount, and a screenshot of the charge. Our team investigates disputes within 10 business days and will issue a correction if an error is confirmed.
